Simply select a movie, choose your preferred theatre and showtime, select your seats, and complete the booking process.

Can I cancel my booking? +

Cancellation depends on the movie, theatre, and booking policy. Please check the cancellation terms before confirming your booking.

I have a payment problem. What should I do? +

If your payment was deducted but your ticket was not confirmed, contact our support team with your booking details.
